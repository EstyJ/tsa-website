Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Click "Register" in the navigation menu, fill in your details, verify your email and phone number, then submit. Your account will be created, but course access requires payment confirmation from our admin team.

We offer two payment methods: instant online payment via Paystack (card, bank transfer, or USSD), or manual bank transfer with admin confirmation. After registering, you'll see both options on your dashboard.

Teachers don't self-register. Visit our Careers page, fill out the application form, and upload your CV. Our admin team reviews applications and creates accounts for successful applicants.

Live classes are powered by Jitsi Meet, embedded directly into your dashboard. No separate app download is needed — just log in and join when your teacher goes live.

Online payments via Paystack are confirmed instantly. Manual bank transfers are typically confirmed by our admin team within 24 hours of proof of payment being submitted.
